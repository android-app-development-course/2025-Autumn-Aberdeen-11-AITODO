# AITODO

**AITODO** is a next-generation task management tool powered by deep AI integration. It transcends the traditional "passive recording" model, serving as a proactive "thinking partner" that helps you plan, decompose, and execute your goals.

## ✨ Core Highlights

-   **🤖 Intelligent Task Decomposition**: Simply enter a broad goal (e.g., "Plan a week-long trip to Japan"), and the AI will automatically break it down into specific, actionable sub-tasks.
-   **💬 Conversational Interface**: Manage your life through a natural chat UI. AITODO listens, understands, and organizes your thoughts into structured plans.
-   **🛡️ Privacy-First (BYOK)**: AITODO follows the **Bring Your Own Key** model. You provide your own AI provider's API Key (OpenAI, Claude, etc.), ensuring your data remains private and giving you full control over which model to use.
-   **🧠 Proactive Context Awareness**: The AI analyzes conversation context to identify potential tasks and suggests adding them before you even realize you've missed something.

## 🚀 Key Features

-   **Robust Task Engine**: A reliable "external memory" for creating, tagging, prioritizing, and archiving tasks.
-   **Smart Scheduling**: Get personalized suggestions for your daily agenda based on task urgency and your historical productivity patterns.
-   **Structured Data Mapping**: Utilizing advanced Prompt Engineering, AITODO transforms AI responses into structured JSON entities, ensuring every chat interaction results in precise task management.

## 🛠️ How it Works

1.  **Configure**: Input your preferred AI provider's API Endpoint and Key in settings.
2.  **Interact**: Type or speak your intentions in natural language.
3.  **Process**: The system guides the AI to output structured data (JSON).
4.  **Sync**: The app parses the data and instantly creates or updates tasks within your list.

## 🏁 Get Started

1. Install AITODO.
2. Setup your `API Key` in **Settings > AI Configuration**.
3. Type: *"I want to start learning Python daily."* and watch AITODO build your curriculum.
